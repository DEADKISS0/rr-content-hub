# RR Video Editor prototype: upstream attribution

This prototype adapts the open-source video editor at [storytold/filmcraft](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft), based on upstream commit `f4090d7b34`.

The upstream source is offered under **MIT OR Apache-2.0**. The corresponding upstream license files and asset notices are retained in this project. `ATTRIBUTION.md` records the bundled third-party assets and their licenses.

This RR prototype changes the visible product identity and removes the upstream ArtCraft wordmark assets as required by the upstream repository rules. It is not an official FilmCraft or ArtCraft release. The `filmcraft` project/file-format identifiers and browser API names remain where needed for compatibility; they refer to the upstream implementation, not the RR product identity.
